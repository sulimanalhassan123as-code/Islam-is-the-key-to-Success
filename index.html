Islam is the key.
Success follows the key.

A calm, plain introduction: what Muslims believe, what is asked of them, and how to begin reading the Qur'an today without paying anybody.

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

“There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.” This single sentence is what makes a person a Muslim: a testimony said with understanding and intent.

The five pillars

The framework every Muslim builds a life on.

1

Shahādah

The testimony that there is no god but Allah and Muhammad is His Messenger.

2

Ṣalāh

Five daily prayers: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.

3

Zakāh

An annual 2.5% of qualifying savings given to those in need.

4

Ṣawm

Fasting the month of Ramadan from dawn to sunset.

5

Ḥajj

The pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those able.

The six articles of faith

What a Muslim believes in the heart.

Allah

One God, without partner, child or equal. The Most Merciful.

The angels

Created beings who carry out Allah's command, including Jibrīl who brought revelation.

The books

Including the Qur'an, the final revealed book, preserved in its original Arabic.

The messengers

From Adam through Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā and ʿĪsā, ending with Muhammad ﷺ.

The Last Day

Resurrection, accounting, and the final destination of Paradise or Fire.

Divine decree

Nothing happens outside Allah's knowledge and will, and people still choose and are accountable.

Why it holds

Three claims Islam makes, and where to check them.

1.
One God, no intermediaries. No priest stands between you and your Creator, and no fee is charged for forgiveness.
“And your Lord says: call upon Me, I will respond to you.”Qur'an 40:60
2.
The Qur'an is preserved and testable. One text, one recitation tradition, memorised by millions. Read it and judge for yourself.
“Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an, and indeed, We will be its guardian.”Qur'an 15:9
3.
The Prophet's life is documented. His words and actions were recorded with chains of narration, so you can examine them instead of taking anyone's word.
“Indeed in the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example.”Qur'an 33:21

Start here, in this order

No money, no membership, no middleman.

1.
Read a short surah with its meaning. Begin with Al-Fātiḥah, then Al-Ikhlāṣ. Understanding first, memorising later.
2.
Learn the prayer. Five prayers, short and fixed. Learn one rak'ah properly before adding the rest.
3.
Ask a real teacher. Find a local imam or a recognised institution. For rulings on your own situation, ask a qualified scholar, not a search engine.
